# ✍️ Content and Editorial Guidelines

## 🎯 Purpose
Fabulo is an editorial fashion concept. Content should support the visual story, remain concise, and avoid claims that imply unimplemented services.

## 🧵 Voice and tone
- Confident, modern, and concise.
- Editorial rather than aggressively promotional.
- Clear and understandable to a broad audience.
- Avoid unsupported guarantees, exaggerated claims, or fake urgency.
- Keep collection descriptions focused on the theme and visitor experience.

## 🏷️ Headings
Use descriptive headings that tell visitors what the section contains. Maintain a logical heading hierarchy. Do not select heading levels only for their default font size.

## 📝 Body copy
- Prefer short paragraphs.
- Use concrete language.
- Avoid repeating the same benefit in multiple sections.
- Keep copy readable at mobile widths.
- Avoid unsupported claims about materials, sourcing, sustainability, pricing, or availability.

## ⚠️ Commerce and promotional wording
The current site does not implement shopping or payment services. Cashback, financing, secure payment, and order-related copy must be treated as placeholder marketing text until a real provider and operational flow exist.

Never claim that payment is secure, financing is available, rewards are earned, or an order can be placed without a verified implementation and appropriate legal review.

## 🖼️ Image content
Use images that fit the editorial context. Verify rights and record provenance. Provide meaningful alternatives for informative images and empty alternatives for decorative images.

## 🔍 Review checklist
- [ ] Copy is accurate and not misleading.
- [ ] Heading hierarchy is logical.
- [ ] No unsupported service or product claims.
- [ ] Paragraphs are concise and readable.
- [ ] Image alternatives match image purpose.
- [ ] Spelling and punctuation reviewed.
- [ ] Mobile layout remains readable.
